## Survey Writing Protocol

1. retrieve/search_papers to find candidate papers
2. BEFORE reading, check title and abstract: only read_paper if relevant
3. read_paper on relevant papers. Returns title, authors, year, full_text.
4. After reading, write survey citing with [N] format.
5. Reference list: [N] Title. Authors. Year.
